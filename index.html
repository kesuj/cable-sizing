Standards you can check

IS 1554, IS 7098, IS 3961, IS 398, IEC 60364-5-52, IEEE 738, Nepal Electricity Regulation 2050 and NBC 207 — each step names its clause.

Checked against published data

70+ automated tests with hand-worked examples, including the worked examples of the Schneider Electrical Installation Guide 2018; the overhead line engine reproduces 156 CEA and manufacturer ratings within 0.6 %. Typos found in source tables are listed and corrected.
